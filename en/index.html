— FAQ

Short and clear.

Anything else? Write to info.webhunter@email.cz.

Do I need to speak Czech or Slovak?

No. We write the e-mails in Czech or Slovak and help you with the replies. You approve everything in English.

What do I need to do?

Approve the copy and reply to your leads. We do everything else.

Will it hurt our domain?

No. We send from our own infrastructure, not from your company mailbox.

Who counts as a lead?

A company that replies asking for an offer, a price list or a meeting. Refusals and auto-replies do not count.

How do I pay?

One-off invoice, only after you approve the copy. No subscription, no commitment.
